The line fold

Fold a sheet, hammer the folded edge, anneal it, then open it up. Hammering stretches the edge, so it ends up longer than the metal behind it. The folded packet curls, and when you open it that curl becomes a raised, arching spine. Put more blows where you want more curve.

    About

    Inspired by the fold-formed metalwork of Greg Greenwood. Fold forming as a technique was developed by Charles Lewton-Brain. The physics here is a simplified model meant to build intuition, not to predict a real piece exactly. Keys: A anneal, Z with Ctrl/⌘ undo.
