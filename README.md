# 30dayassignment

Projects made for the 30-day assignment, one folder each. The home page links to all of them: https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Tuxedo Tail](tuxedo-tail/) | A 3D browser toy: stroke a tuxedo cat from head to tail tip and its tail grows a little each time. |
| [InstaFace](instaface/) | A big-text social feed for older people. Everything works, but posts stay on your own device and pretend friends reply. |
| [Silverbench](silverbench/) | A silversmithing bench simulator: cut, solder, rivet and finish sterling on a soldering board, with guided lessons and 3D views of hinges and clasps. |
| [Foldline](foldline/) | A fold-forming simulator and design bench: fold a blank, hammer the edge, anneal and open it, and watch the form rise in 3D. Inspired by Greg Greenwood. |
| [Goblet Works](goblet-works/) | A 3D glassblowing sandbox: gather, blow, pull a stem, paddle a foot and open the rim to make goblets, then keep them on a shelf. |
| [Clean Bones](clean-bones/) | An osteology prep game: clean a rabbit skeleton with beetles, superworms or maceration, then degrease, whiten and lay it out for grading. |
| [Carcass](carcass/) | A specimen prep simulator that starts with a whole raccoon: skin, gut, deflesh, bag the small bones and macerate, with every mistake carried to a museum grade. |
| [Vitrigraph Lab](vitrigraph-lab/) | A vitrigraph kiln learning game: load a pot with colored glass, heat it, pull cane by hand, then chop it to see the murrini cross-sections. A bonus module for Before The Heat. |
| [Glass Blocks](glass-blocks/) | Build glass objects from shape presets that stack on a center axis, stretch, squash and taper them, and see them rendered as glass in 3D. [Play it online](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/glass-blocks/play/). |
| [Jackline](jackline/) | Draw half a vessel's profile and it spins into a hollow 3D glass piece: tumblers, goblets, pitchers, bottles. Checks capacity, weight, grip, cleaning and tipping, and prints a 1:1 profile template. [Play it online](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/jackline/play/). |
| [Glass CAD](glass-cad/) | Part CAD, part playground for glass: size parts in inches or centimetres, stack, cut, melt, shell and deform them, see them as glass in 3D, and print a measured shop sheet. [Play it online](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/glass-cad/play/). |
| [Glass Mash](glass-mash/) | A glass sketchbook: draw shapes flat and they inflate into 3D glass you can move, melt together and carve. [Play it online](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/glass-mash/play/). |
| [Finial](finial/) | A planning tool for hand-blown lidded jars: draw the body and lid profiles, check wall thickness, lid fit, capacity and weight, and print a shop sheet for the bench. [Play it online](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/finial/play/). |
| [Flacon](flacon/) | A planning tool for hand-blown perfume bottles: draw the body, size the neck from a ground stopper, cork or bought hardware, check capacity in ml with a fill line, resize to a target, and flag a heavy base or thin neck. Shares Finial's vessel core. [Play it online](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/flacon/play/). |
| [Pulled Pony](pony/) | A one-page hot-glass toy: pull legs, neck, head and tail from a gather on the punty before it cools, reheat in the glory hole, and anneal it to a PNG. [Play it online](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/pony/). |
| [Paint Mixer](paint-mixer/) | Mixes a target color from the opaque paints you actually own: photograph a swatch sheet so it measures your paints, sample colors from a reference photo, get recipes in whole parts, then photograph your mix and get told how to fix it. Works offline on iPhone. [Open it](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/paint-mixer/). |
| [Hot Shop Atlas](hot-shop-atlas/) | A free map and directory of US glassblowing studios, drawn as a glass colour-bar chart: every studio is a numbered swatch, each checked against the studio's own website. Search, filter, share links and "Near me". [Open it](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/hot-shop-atlas/). |
| [Armature](armature/) | A metal + glass sculpture builder: build a frame from rods and cages, hang glass on clips, bolts and wire wraps, blow glass into a wire cage, and check weight, balance and cold connections. [Play it online](https://billyl0uis.github.io/30dayassignment/armature/play/). |
